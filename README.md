# 📱 AutoMate — Your Smart Car Maintenance Companion

AutoMate is a mobile app that helps car owners effortlessly track and manage their vehicle's maintenance — no more forgetting oil changes, renewals, or service intervals.

The app intelligently calculates when your car will next need service based on your last maintenance, mileage, and usage patterns, then sends timely reminders before it's due. Everything from oil changes to insurance renewals — managed automatically in one simple app.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚗 Car Profiles

Add and manage multiple cars with details like model, year, mileage, and plate number.

### 🛠️ Maintenance Tracker

Log your last maintenance (oil, tires, battery, etc.) and AutoMate auto-calculates the next due date.

### 🔔 Smart Reminders

Get push notifications or WhatsApp messages before upcoming maintenance, renewals, or inspections.

### 📊 Maintenance History

Keep a complete log of past services, costs, and notes — perfect for tracking expenses or selling your car.

### 📅 License & Insurance Alerts

Never miss a renewal — set reminders for license, insurance, or inspection dates.

### 🤖 (Coming Soon) AI Insights

AutoMate learns your driving habits to predict when your car might need service before issues arise.

## 💡 Our Goal

To save car owners time, money, and stress by automating maintenance tracking — ensuring every car stays in top condition without the hassle of manual follow-ups.
